# Bundled assets

| File | What | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `spark.svg`, `atlas-icon-64.png` | The app's own marks. | Same as the app. |

The OFL allows bundling and embedding a font in an app; the font is not sold on its own and keeps its name. Recursive (the app's other display face) comes from the `@fontsource-variable/recursive` package, also OFL.
